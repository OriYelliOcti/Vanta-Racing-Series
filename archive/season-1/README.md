# Season 1 — 2026

Results, standings and the champion of the first Vanta season, kept as they were when the
season closed. The live site reads `data/`, not this folder; nothing here is served or
edited after the fact.

When the season ends, copy `data/results.json`, `data/lineups.json` and `data/news.json`
in here under their final names, plus `champion.md` with the drivers' and teams' titles.
